🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how What2Eat ("we", "our", or "the site") handles information when you visit https://barrystlow.github.io/what2eat/

Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. What2Eat has no accounts, no sign-up, and no databases.

Local Storage

When you add custom options to the wheel, they are saved using your browser's local storage. This data:

  • Stays entirely on your own device
  • Is never sent to us or any third party
  • Can be erased at any time by clearing your browser data

Third-Party Links

The "Find Nearby" feature opens Google Maps in a new tab using your device's location, which you explicitly allow. We never see or store your location. Google's own Privacy Policy applies once you leave our site.

Advertising

We may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising via Google Ads Settings.

Children's Privacy

What2Eat is safe for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Reach us via the Contact page.
