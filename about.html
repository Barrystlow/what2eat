🍽️ About What2Eat

What2Eat is a free, fun decision-making tool for one of life's smallest daily struggles: "What should I eat?"

We've all stood in front of the fridge or scrolled through food apps for 20 minutes and still couldn't decide. What2Eat solves that with a simple, colorful spinning wheel. Pick a preset category — Fast Food, Healthy, Asian, Italian, Breakfast, or Desserts — add your own options, give the wheel a spin, and let fate decide your next meal.

Why we built it

Decision fatigue is real. Small choices like picking a meal drain mental energy that could be spent on bigger things. What2Eat turns a moment of indecision into a fun little game — and gets you eating faster.

What makes it different

  • No sign-up required — just open and spin
  • Your options stay yours — custom entries are saved privately in your own browser, never on our servers
  • Instant nearby suggestions — after the wheel picks, one tap takes you to Google Maps to find that food near you
  • Completely free
